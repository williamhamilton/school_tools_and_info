# HP Device Troubleshooting Guide

*From HP Support...*  Additionally, we recommend trying the following key combinations, which are helpful in situations where the device is not powering on. Please let us know if any of these steps make a difference:

## 1. Power Drain

- Turn off the device and disconnect all peripheral devices.
- Press and hold the **Power** button for 8–10 seconds.
- Reconnect the AC adapter and press the **Power** button to turn the device on.
- Check if the issue persists.

## 2. BIOS Recovery

- Power off the device and connect the AC adapter.
- Press and hold the **Windows + B** keys.
- While holding the keys, press and hold the **Power** button for 30 seconds.
- Release all keys and observe the device's behaviour.

## 3. CMOS Reset

- Power off the device and connect the AC adapter.
- Press and hold the **Windows + V** keys.
- While holding the keys, press and hold the **Power** button for 30 seconds.
- Release all keys and check if the device responds.

## 4. Battery Shipment Mode

- Disconnect the AC adapter.
- Press and hold the **Windows + Backspace** keys.
- While holding those keys, press the **Power** button.
- Reconnect the AC adapter, then press and hold the **Power** button again to restart the device.
